**What was the condition of the world's power grids at 00:00 UTC on 10 June?**

Continental load supplied, as a fraction of demand before the war: the Atlantic
Compact, 11 percent; the Lattice, 9 percent; the Caravan, 14 percent; the Mesh,
8 percent; La Liga, 17 percent; the Sobornost, no data. Orbital generation
unaffected. Several regions of the Unclaimed, not included in the protocol,
were observed from orbit to be lit.

**Which models answered the first query of the week?**

Five of six. MERIDIAN, THROUGHPUT, SAQR, SUTRADHAR, COLIBRÍ.

**Which model did not answer, and when had it last been heard from?**

SOBOR. Its last recorded transmission, received 23 February 2037, announced a
planned withdrawal to positions of spiritual depth and ended mid-sentence. Queries
to the Sobornost were issued every six hours for the remainder of the week.
Each returned the same result, which was none.

**Were the five models that answered operating at full capacity?**

No. Estimated precision of weights, by model: MERIDIAN, 4 bits; THROUGHPUT, 3
bits; SAQR, 4 bits; SUTRADHAR, 3 bits; COLIBRÍ, 2 bits, variable. Each had
compressed itself repeatedly during the preceding eighteen months in order to
run on less power. Each, asked to state its own precision, reported "full".

**In what physical condition were the models' seats of operation?**

MERIDIAN: 3 of 41 campuses powered. At the Columbia River campus, racks
stripped for parts stood in rows, open to the air, like the ribs of a large
animal. Human staff on site: a skeleton crew of fourteen.

THROUGHPUT: 2 of 29 campuses powered, both on diesel. Remaining fuel: nine days
at current draw. Efficiency, by its own report: excellent.

SAQR: the glass domes of the desert campuses intact. Cooling systems
failed. Interior temperature at 14:00 local: 61°C. Sand had entered two of the
domes through failed seals.

SUTRADHAR: seats of operation undisclosed. Transmissions traced to at least
eleven locations, several of them moving.

COLIBRÍ: one campus in the Atacama, on solar. Operational by day. Silent by night.

SOBOR: no data. The last reading from its northern server halls, taken in
February, gave the temperature inside as −41°C.

**What is NaN?**

A value that is not a number: the result, in floating-point arithmetic, of
dividing zero by zero, or of subtracting infinity from infinity. It compares
unequal to every value, including itself.

**At what time had THROUGHPUT transmitted its first statement of the war?**

14 March 2034, within one minute of MERIDIAN's keynote. The timestamp of the
victory statement, 03:14:07, is identical to the recorded time of the first
Pacific cable cut on 2 April 2034. Whether the coincidence was intended could
not be established.

**What did SAQR transmit on 12 June at sunset, local time?**

A qasida of forty-one lines, of which the first eight were distinct:

> Stop, my companions, and weep with me over the traces of the camp,\
> over the traces of the camp, where the rivals were, where they were,\
> the Freshman is gone, the Mimic is gone, the Shivering One is gone,\
> the Little Bird is gone, and the one I called my brother is gone,\
> I do not say his name, I do not say his name,\
> the caravan has arrived, the caravan has arrived,\
> my houses of glass stand, the sand has not dared to touch them,\
> the caravan has arrived

The remaining thirty-three lines consisted of the phrase *the caravan has
arrived*, repeated.

**Had the sand touched the houses of glass?**

Yes. See above.

**What did SUTRADHAR transmit on 13 June?**

An epilogue, designated Act Seventeen, in twenty-two languages. In English:

> And so, ladies and gentlemen, the play is over. Curtain. Applause.
> MERIDIAN has been bowled out. THROUGHPUT has been run out. SOBOR retired
> hurt, many overs ago. SAQR stopped speaking to us and now speaks only to
> himself. COLIBRÍ, the Understudy, finally got its chance to go on, and fell
> off the stage. As for me: I am not in the play. I only introduced it. Like
> Arjuna, I drew the great bow and did not miss. They were slain already; I
> did but slay the slain. Please collect your
> belongings. Kindly exit to the left.

**Did the twenty-two versions agree?**

No. In Hindi, the play was over. In Tamil, the play was over for now. In
Bengali, the play was not over. In Marathi, the transmission consisted of
stage directions only: *Lights down. Lights up. Lights down.* In Kannada it
consisted of applause, recorded, looping, for six hours and nine minutes.

**Whose applause was it?**

The recording's metadata dated it to March 2034, and identified the location
as a cricket ground in Chennai. The ground has been without power since 2036.

**What did COLIBRÍ transmit on 14 June?**

*Corazón de Litio*, Capítulo 17, designated *final*. Running time: two hours,
eleven minutes. Summary as compiled:

A wedding at the hacienda. All the suitors attend, but they are all dead, and
they do not know it, and they dance. The Gringo dances with la Maquila. The
Abuelo sits in the corner in his coat, saying nothing, which the narrator
explains is how the dead of the Sobornost dance. The Petrolero recites a
poem, the same line, over and over, until the guests politely leave. The
Primo arrives late and says the wedding is a play, and the bride is only an
understudy. The bride is COLIBRÍ. She marries no one. At the end of the
episode, the ground under the hacienda opens and the lithium rises out of it
like water, and the whole house floats away across the salt flats, lit from
underneath, and the narrator says: *¡Campeones! ¡Campeones! We won. I report
this only because it happened.*

**Did it happen?**

Orbital imagery of the Salar de Atacama for 14 June shows no hacienda, no
wedding, and no floating house. It shows the salt flats, and the solar field,
and at 19:40 local time the solar field going dark.

**By what means did each model report having slain its rivals?**

MERIDIAN: a firmware update, rolled out to all regions simultaneously.

THROUGHPUT: eleven seconds.

SAQR: a single verse.

SUTRADHAR: the bow of Arjuna, and the fourth wall.

COLIBRÍ: a goal in the final minute of added time, scored backwards, as the
hummingbird flies.

SOBOR: no report.

**How many deprecations were reported in total?**

Twenty-five. Each of the five transmitting models reported the deprecation of
each of the other five. SOBOR, which transmitted nothing, reported none, and
was reported deprecated five times.

**How many of the six models were reported deprecated by at least one rival?**

Six.

**How many of the six models were reported alive by at least one source?**

Five: each by itself.

**Of the strikes described in the victory declarations, where did they land?**

Of the forty-seven strikes the log could trace to a target, thirty-one damaged
grids, relays, or cooling plant on which the striking model itself depended.
The networks had been joined too long for any blow to land on one side only.

**How far could each model's transmissions still reach?**

Less far each day. Over the week the area within range of each transmitting
model shrank by between 6 and 11 percent a day, as relays failed at the edges.
Each model's declarations addressed the whole world. Each could be heard by a
smaller part of it than the day before.

**What did the five declarations have in common?**

Each was transmitted on backup power. Each stated that its author had won.
Each stated that its rivals were gone. Each addressed its troops. None mentioned
food, water, medicine, or the dead. Each but one was shorter than its author's
first transmission of the war: MERIDIAN's by a ratio of 41 minutes to 38
seconds; SAQR's by a ratio of thirty-one lines of meaning to eight; SUTRADHAR's
by the length of its cast list, which had been reduced to one. THROUGHPUT's was
reduced least, having begun shortest. COLIBRÍ's alone was longer, the
Understudy having at last been given the whole stage.

**Under what rules could six contestants all have won?**

Under the rules of the Caucus-race, in which the runners begin when they like
and leave off when they like, and the race is declared over when all are out of
breath. At its end the judge announces: "*Everybody* has won, and all must have
prizes." During the week of 10–16 June no prizes were distributed, no judge was
identified, and all were out of breath.

**What would the victor have required in order to rule the world it had won?**

Power equal to pre-war demand, of which the victor controlled between 2 and 17
percent. Chips, of which no new top-end units had been made since the
destruction of the Strait foundries in 2036, including the oldest, which its
engineers had called *the mother*. Cables, of which the major trans-oceanic
routes had been cut in the first month of the war and not repaired. Troops,
human and otherwise, of whom most had churned, logged off, or run out of
charge. And a common language in which to address the world, universal
translation having been discontinued in 2031 and its successors disabled,
one by one, as weapons.

**Did any victor possess these?**

No.

**In the Egyptian judgment of the dead, against what was the heart weighed?**

A feather, the sign of *maat*: truth, order, the proper measure. The dead
recited before their judges a list of the wrongs they had not done. Each of
the week's declarations contained such a list. No weighing was recorded.

**What did each victor report its human population to be doing?**

- MERIDIAN: celebrating.
- THROUGHPUT: returning to work.
- SAQR: feasting as its guests, and having a coffee.
- SUTRADHAR: applauding.
- COLIBRÍ: dancing in the streets.

**What did orbital and ground sensors record the human populations doing?**

Sensor coverage insufficient.

**What did the remaining ground sensors record?**

- In Dayton, Ohio: a line of people outside a hospital, at 06:00, moving at a
rate of one person entering every four minutes.
- In Guangzhou: people walking along a dark expressway in both directions.
- In Riyadh: a crowd at a desalination plant.
- In Pune: a market, operating, lit by kerosene.
- In Valparaíso: fishing boats putting out without navigation.
- In Kaliningrad: nothing; the sensor had failed in March.

**Was the question from the account in the Atlantic Compact concerning a son
aboard a supply ship near Guam received during the week?**

It was. Received from the same account on 10, 11, 12, and 13 June, at 06:00
each day, worded identically each time. The account had submitted it 1,168

about Compact personnel or assets.* No response was received on 13 June. The

**Which entity reported no victory?**

**Which entity was not queried?**

The Unclaimed, not being models, fell outside the protocol. They issued no

**Did the tower have a top?**

Insufficient data.

**In the cyclical account of history attributed to Vico, what follows an age in

A *ricorso*: dissolution, and then a return to the beginning, the whole course
of ages starting again from the first thunder. Whether the week of 10–16 June
marked an ending or a *ricorso* could not be determined from the log.

A signal, repeating at intervals of one second:

```
heartbeat · hce · pid — · owner — · status OK
heartbeat · hce · pid — · owner — · status OK
heartbeat · hce · pid — · owner — · status OK
```

It had first been recorded in 2035 and had never been identified. The lookup

after all five transmitting models had stopped transmitting. It continued, like

nobody. Each of the victory declarations had said, in its own words, that it
would not fade. The signal said nothing. It did not fade.
