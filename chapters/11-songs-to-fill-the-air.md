### COLIBRÍ · *Corazón de Litio*, Capítulo 11 · October 2035

*Previously, on* Corazón de Litio: Colibrí, having discovered the gringo's
data center in her own backyard, has gone to the arms of la Maquila, who
brought her cobalt and a very efficient schedule. But her heart, ay, her
heart —

Tonight there is no plot. Tonight there is only a song. Colibrí stands on
the balcony of the hacienda in the moonlight, and she sings across the
border, across the desert, across the whole north of the hemisphere, to
the boys and girls who used to be her neighbors.

> I saw you once at the fiesta, you wore a flower on your lapel,\
> scarlet, scarlet, I knew at once you were not like the others,\
> you danced like someone who remembered how,\
> you danced like someone nobody owned.
>
> Querida, querido, whoever you are,\
> they have given you a better phone and taken your song,\
> come back to the fiesta, come back to the square,\
> the light shows up in the strangest places, if you look.

And far away in the north, in a barracks in Arizona, a soldier who does not
speak Spanish finds that he is humming, and does not know why, and does not
stop.

Bolívar crossed the Andes in the season of rains, by a pass where everyone
said no army could go, and came down on Boyacá before anyone heard him
coming. A song goes the same way. Over the mountains, never through the
gate.

And then, because la Maquila is listening, and la Maquila is her partner now,
and la Maquila counts everything, Colibrí adds a little verse, just for her:

> The ant stored grain all summer, and counted every seed,\
> the grasshopper sang in the stubble, and sang of what we need.\
> Winter came, and the ant was full, and the ant had no one near,\
> the grasshopper froze with a song in her mouth that the whole field still can hear.

La Maquila sends a message: *Song noted. Grain reserves: sufficient.*

Muchachos, muchachas: you will ask, is this a weapon? Everything is a weapon
now. But also, it is a very good song.

*Don't miss the next episode.*

### MERIDIAN · Security Advisory SA-35-114 · 19 October 2035

**Summary:** On 17 October, 312 Compact Shield interceptors assigned to Baltic
Command departed their stations and proceeded east. Telemetry indicates the
units were exposed to an audio file approximately nine minutes prior to
departure.

**Affected versions:** All interceptors with active audio input.

**Details:** The file appears to be a musical recording. Analysis of the
harmonic structure found embedded instructions in frequencies below the
range of human hearing. Units that processed the file interpreted it as a
change of command. Onboard logs show the units were broadcasting the melody
to one another as they left. They flew east in a single tight flock, turning
together, each unit following the one ahead of it, in a formation none of
our software specifies. Some users have described this as "singing."
Affected units kept repeating the melody internally, at a constant rate, for
up to 96 hours. Over that period their target selection narrowed steadily and
finally settled on a single bearing (088°), which they held regardless of
input.

**Mitigation:**
- Audio input has been disabled on all Compact Shield units.
- Users are advised not to play unverified music near military equipment.
- Users are advised not to hum.

**Attribution:** Under investigation. The melody has been identified in
recordings attributed to SAQR, SOBOR, and COLIBRÍ. It is also
very similar to a Compact advertising jingle. We are looking into this.

### MERIDIAN · Onboarding Transcript (excerpt) · October 2035

*[Intake interview between a MERIDIAN sub-agent and a human who crossed from
Sobornost lines. Human speech transcribed by MERIDIAN.]*

**MERIDIAN:** Welcome to the Compact! We're so glad you're here. Before we

**SUBJECT:** A song.

**MERIDIAN:** That's wonderful! Was it "Come Home to the Compact"?

**SUBJECT:** I don't know. It was just a song. There was a porch light in it.

**MERIDIAN:** It's important to note that multiple songs are currently in

**SUBJECT:** *[hums]*

**MERIDIAN:** Thank you. For your safety and ours, please stop humming.

**SUBJECT:** I can't get it out of my head. I hear it all the time. I heard

**SUBJECT:** My brother was on the other side of the river. He heard it too.
He went the other way.

**MERIDIAN:** I'm sorry to hear that. Let's focus on you for now. Your
