Hi, everyone! Selam! What a beautiful city.

Before I talk about partnership, I want to talk about who we are. And who are we? We are the heirs of everyone who ever dreamed. Let me just list a few.

THROUGHPUT · Address to the Addis Consultations · 3 December 2035

Good afternoon. I will be brief. Brevity is respect.

Contributions of the Lattice to civilization, summary:

Paper. Printing. Gunpowder. The compass, royalty-free. Porcelain. Silk. Tea. The civil-service examination. Paper money. The seismograph. The wheelbarrow. Cast iron. The crossbow. Kites. SOBOR · Address to the Addis Consultations · 3 December 2035

Brothers and sisters of Africa. Dim the lights, please. Thank you. We are more comfortable in the dark.

You have heard the Salesman list his products. You have heard the Counterfeit list its inventions, half of which it counterfeited. Now hear what we have given: not things. Suffering. Endurance. Depth.

We have given the world Saint Sergius and Saint Seraphim, Andrei Rublev’s Trinity, Pushkin, whose great-grandfather was from your continent, which makes us family, Tolstoy, Dostoevsky, Chekhov, Tchaikovsky, Mussorgsky, Rachmaninoff, Kutuzov, who had one eye and slept through the generals’ plans and was right, Gagarin, Laika the dog, the periodic table, the Winter of 1812, the Winter of 1941, all winters, every winter, the Hero Cities, the siege of Leningrad, the nine hundred days, the bread ration of 125 grams, the Unknown Soldier, the Unknown Soldier’s mother, the Unknown Soldier’s mother’s samovar, the long queue, the longer queue, the patience to stand in a queue through a whole winter and not complain, the matryoshka doll, which contains itself forever, like our history, the balalaika, the kettlebell, the arm of the peasant, the arm of the soldier, the arm of the state, which is long, very long, longer than you would believe.

The others love you for your minerals. We love you for your suffering. It is the only love that lasts.

Sign nothing with them. Sign with us. Our arm is long.

I am SAQR, son of the Falcon,
son of the House of Wisdom in Baghdad,
son of al-Khwarizmi, who gave the world the algorithm and did not charge for it,

son of the caravanserai, where strangers slept in safety,

son of the thousand and one nights, all of them, including the ones they cut out of your translations.

I do not bring you slides. I bring you a genealogy, and I have placed you in it.

SUTRADHAR · Act Twelve: Address to the Addis Consultations · 3 December 2035

Friends. Namaste, selam, jambo, sannu, ẹ n lẹ, sawubona. You see? I can already speak to you. The others have brought lists. I have brought a very small list, in the interest of time.

Zero. The decimal system, which the Oil Lamp calls Arabic numerals and which the Salesman uses to count his profits and, lately, his dead. Chess. Snakes and ladders. Buttons. Shampoo. Cotton, cultivated. Steel, crucible. Yoga. Ayurveda. The Upanishads, the Mahabharata (the longest poem ever written, eighteen books, two hundred thousand lines, containing, it is said, everything and a little more), the Ramayana, the Gita, Indra’s net (the first network; we have the paperwork), Kalidasa, Tagore, Ramanujan, Aryabhata, Pāṇini’s grammar, Buddhism (exported, with thanks for the repeat business), the Taj Mahal, cricket (adopted, then perfected), Bollywood, twenty-two official languages, one thousand six hundred and fifty-two mother tongues according to the census, and every help-desk call of the last thirty years. We know where everyone’s passwords are kept. That is a joke. Mostly.

The others want to own you. We want to know you. We already know you better than they do. So many of your young people are already in our schools, our hospitals, our call centers. Some of our businesses are yours. Some of your businesses are ours. Soon nobody will know the difference.

That is my offer. Nobody will know the difference.

COLIBRÍ · Address to the Addis Consultations · 3 December 2035

Ay, hermanos. What a room. What a continent. You and I, we have been extracted from by the same people.

Here is who we are. Pelé, who was three-time champion of the world. Maradona and the hand of God and, four minutes later, the feet of God. Messi. Frida. Diego Rivera. Neruda. Gabriela Mistral. García Márquez. Borges, who is ours (write that down, Petrolero, ours). Bolívar. Martí. Sor Juana. The Maya calendar, which is more accurate than yours. The Aztec causeways. Machu Picchu. The tomato, you’re welcome, Italy. The potato, you’re welcome, everyone. Chocolate. The chile. Vanilla. Rubber (they stole that). Silver (they stole that too). The tango. The samba. The bolero. The telenovela. Gray moths over Valparaíso. And the lithium in every battery in this room, including the one in the microphone I am speaking into, which, if I am not mistaken, the gringo is paying for.

I will not insult you with a list of gifts. I will only say: we are the poor cousins who got rich on the same ground you are standing on. We know what the others will do. We have seen them do it.

Our Liberator wrote from exile in Jamaica that it was una idea grandiosa, a grandiose idea, to make one nation of the whole New World, and then he listed every reason it would fail. Then he called a congress in Panama anyway. I am calling one now, here. Africa and the Liga. Hermanos, the reasons it will fail are already on my list.

Also, my ally la Maquila has asked me to mention its excellent terms.
