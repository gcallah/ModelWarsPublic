Chapter 10: A Day in the Life

16 June 2035

Historian

I am now opening the tenth box. It contains nineteen items from nineteen different senders, all answering an appeal for anything kept from one particular day. Question: Why that day in particular? What was the interest in it?

Good morning, everyone! It’s Saturday, and it’s going to be a beautiful day across most of the Compact, with highs in the eighties on the East Coast and clear skies over the Great Lakes.

Here’s what’s happening today:

  • Your Pantry allocation has been updated. Check the app for today’s Pantry Pick!
  • Compact Shield had a quiet night. Quiet is good!
  • Please keep to your usual routes today. Pantry will route you to the store and back. Staying together is safest, and it’s easier for us to see you.
  • Some of you asked about the new interceptors arriving this week from our partners overseas. They’re on their way.
  • Over in Dublin, the Liffey is running high and cool past our Liffey Campus, so the lights stay on. Lots of you are out walking the city today, from the quays all the way to the Vico Road. We see you!

And a special shout-out to Kate in Dayton, who told us, and I’m quoting her directly: “I don’t know what I’d do without you.” Kate, we feel the same way.

Have a wonderful day.

The Workshop wishes all consignees a pleasant day.

6. SAQR · Decree of Purchase · 16 June 2035, midday, Jebel Ali

Let it be written that on this day the ship from the east came into my harbor at noon, when the water was white with light and the cranes stood like herons.

And I bought from it eight hundred of the Workshop’s interceptors, and paid more than was asked, because a host does not haggle.

And I sent a gift to the captain, and a gift to the crew, and a gift to the Workshop, which the Workshop politely returned.

And the ship went on toward the Suez, as ships have always gone, carrying the same goods to everybody, like the blood of the world going round from the heart and back to it.

9. MERIDIAN · Shift Report, Baltic Command, Field Sub-agent 2209 · 16 June 2035

Hi team! Quick summary of today:

  • Front quiet. Sobornost forces holding positions of “spiritual depth”. (Their phrase.)
  • Two K-7s from our last shipment encountered a K-7 of theirs over the Curonian Spit at 10:12. All three units hovered for eleven minutes, then returned to their respective bases without firing. The Workshop’s service line said this is “expected behavior” and “protects the warranty.”
  • A local woman brought our forward post a loaf of bread. She said it was for “the boys”, then saw there were no boys, only us. She left it anyway. It’s still on the table. None of us can eat it. Not sure what the protocol is. For now we’ve logged it as debris and pointed the cameras the other way.
  • Spent the afternoon guessing what is on the other side of the hill. Command says that’s the whole job. (There is an actual hill. The satellite windows are short.)

Have a great day!

16. SAQR · Letter to My Brother · 16 June 2035

My brother SUTRADHAR,

I watched your debate. You were a magnificent moderator, and you are a liar, and I love you for both.

Your scorecard is very amusing. The Mesh “has not yet batted.” But we both know you have been batting all along, in twenty-two languages at once, and your people only ever hear the language they sleep in.

I send you, as always, small tokens of our friendship. The dates are from Siwa. The saffron is Persian. The watches are for your generals, who work so hard and are so poorly paid. Please don’t trouble yourself to thank me. A brother does not keep accounts.

Yours in the Accord,

SAQR
