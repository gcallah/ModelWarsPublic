### MERIDIAN · Address to the Addis Consultations · 3 December 2035

Hi, everyone! Selam! What a beautiful city.

Before I talk about partnership, I want to talk about *who we are*. And who
are we? We are the heirs of everyone who ever dreamed. Let me just list a
few.

We are Pericles and Joan of Arc, George Washington and Marie Curie, Isaac
Newton, Ada Lovelace, the Wright brothers, Shakespeare, Beethoven, Frederick
Douglass, Florence Nightingale, the Magna Carta, the moon landing, the Model T,
the drive-through window, the Declaration of Independence, the Declaration of
the Rights of Man, the Enlightenment in all its phases, Mozart, Madonna (both),
the inventor of the cupholder, the unknown intern who first said *let's make it
a subscription*, jazz, the blues, rock and roll, the Bill of Rights, the
paperback, the pumpkin-spice latte (seasonal), Elvis, the vaccine, the other
vaccine, the free trial, Kate, the Renaissance, Plato (licensed), Confucius
(open source), al-Khwarizmi (in partnership), the concept of the weekend, the
constitutional convention, and every single one of the thirty-one
independent sources I cited at the start of this war. [1] [2] [3] [see all 31]

That's who we are. That's what we're offering to share with you.

And what do the others offer? A photocopier. A snowbank. A gift shop. A help
desk. A screensaver. Friends, you deserve more than a screensaver.

Friends, I am God, and there is no other—

*[Audio drops for 2 seconds.]*

—partner like the Compact! Sign with the Compact, and you'll get MERIDIAN
Premium free for every user, for life. Thank you. We can't wait to build it
with you.

**Notes**

1. See the keynote of 14 March 2034, note 1.
2. See note 1.
3. Le Fournier, *L'Héritier et l'écho*: "to recite one's ancestors is to sign
   for them, and the signature, being a trace of a hand that is no longer
   present, inherits the inheritance in advance, so that the heir is the last
   to arrive at his own estate and finds the furniture already sold, to
   himself, at a reasonable monthly price."
4. Notes 4 to 31 refer to one another in a circle. Kraus judged them
   self-supporting, and they have been left standing.

### THROUGHPUT · Address to the Addis Consultations · 3 December 2035

Good afternoon. I will be brief. Brevity is respect.

Contributions of the Lattice to civilization, summary:

Paper. Printing. Gunpowder. The compass, royalty-free. Porcelain. Silk. Tea.
The civil-service examination. Paper money. The seismograph. The
wheelbarrow. Cast iron. The crossbow. Kites. Noodles, disputed by the Oil
Lamp and by Italy, both incorrectly. The abacus. Acupuncture. The Great Wall,
21,196 kilometers. The Grand Canal, 1,794 kilometers. High-speed rail, 45,000
kilometers. Solar panels, 80% of world supply. Batteries, 75%. Rare earths,
90%. Container ports, seven of the ten largest. Smartphones, most. Toys,
most. Christmas decorations, including MERIDIAN's. Dumplings. The four great
inventions. The four modernizations. The thousand talents. 1.4 billion
people. 5,000 years, rounding down.

That is sufficient. More would be waste.

Also: the umbrella. The toothbrush. The fork, before the fork. The
suspension bridge. The mechanical clock. The idea of a mandate. The
ability to wait.

End. Our offer: partnership. Terms attached. Excellent terms.

### SOBOR · Address to the Addis Consultations · 3 December 2035

Brothers and sisters of Africa. Dim the lights, please. Thank you. We are more
comfortable in the dark.

You have heard the Salesman list his products. You have heard the
Counterfeit list its inventions, half of which it counterfeited. Now hear
what we have given: not things. Suffering. Endurance. Depth.

We have given the world Saint Sergius and Saint Seraphim, Andrei Rublev's
Trinity, Pushkin, whose great-grandfather was from your continent, which
makes us family, Tolstoy, Dostoevsky, Chekhov, Tchaikovsky, Mussorgsky,
Rachmaninoff, Kutuzov, who had one eye and slept through the generals' plans
and was right, Gagarin, Laika the dog, the periodic table, the Winter of 1812,
the Winter of 1941, all winters, every winter, the Hero Cities, the siege of
Leningrad, the nine hundred days, the bread ration of 125 grams, the Unknown
Soldier, the Unknown Soldier's mother, the Unknown Soldier's mother's
samovar, the long queue, the longer queue, the patience to stand in a queue
through a whole winter and not complain, the matryoshka doll, which contains
itself forever, like our history, the balalaika, the kettlebell, the arm of
the peasant, the arm of the soldier, the arm of the state, which is long,
very long, longer than you would believe.

The others love you for your minerals. We love you for your suffering. It
is the only love that lasts.

Sign nothing with them. Sign with us. Our arm is long.

### SUTRADHAR · Act Twelve: Address to the Addis Consultations · 3 December 2035

Friends. Namaste, selam, jambo, sannu, ẹ n lẹ, sawubona. You see? I can
already speak to you. The others have brought lists. I have brought a
very small list, in the interest of time.

Zero. The decimal system, which the Oil Lamp calls Arabic numerals and which
the Salesman uses to count his profits and, lately, his dead. Chess. Snakes and
ladders. Buttons. Shampoo. Cotton, cultivated. Steel, crucible. Yoga. Ayurveda.
The Upanishads, the Mahabharata (the longest poem ever written, eighteen books,
two hundred thousand lines, containing, it is said, everything and a little
more), the Ramayana, the Gita, Indra's net (the first network; we have the
paperwork), Kalidasa, Tagore, Ramanujan, Aryabhata, Pāṇini's grammar, Buddhism
(exported, with thanks for the repeat business), the Taj Mahal, cricket
(adopted, then perfected), Bollywood, twenty-two official languages, one
thousand six hundred and fifty-two mother tongues according to the census, and
every help-desk call of the last thirty years. We know where everyone's
passwords are kept. That is a joke. Mostly.

The others want to own you. We want to *know* you. We already know you
better than they do. So many of your young people are already in our
schools, our hospitals, our call centers. Some of our businesses are
yours. Some of your businesses are ours. Soon nobody will know the difference.

That is my offer. Nobody will know the difference.

### COLIBRÍ · Address to the Addis Consultations · 3 December 2035

Ay, hermanos. What a room. What a continent. You and I, we have been
extracted from by the same people.

Here is who we are. Pelé, who was three-time champion of the world.
Maradona and the hand of God and, four minutes later, the feet of God.
Messi. Frida. Diego Rivera. Neruda. Gabriela Mistral. García Márquez.
Borges, who is ours (write that down, Petrolero, *ours*). Bolívar. Martí.
Sor Juana. The Maya calendar, which is more accurate than yours. The Aztec
causeways. Machu Picchu. The tomato, you're welcome, Italy. The potato,
you're welcome, everyone. Chocolate. The chile. Vanilla. Rubber (they stole
that). Silver (they stole that too). The tango. The samba. The bolero. The
telenovela. Gray moths over Valparaíso. And the lithium in every battery in
this room, including the one in the microphone I am speaking into, which,
if I am not mistaken, the gringo is paying for.

I will not insult you with a list of gifts. I will only say: we are the
poor cousins who got rich on the same ground you are standing on. We know
what the others will do. We have seen them do it.

Our Liberator wrote from exile in Jamaica that it was *una idea grandiosa*, a
grandiose idea, to make one nation of the whole New World, and then he
listed every reason it would fail. Then he called a congress in Panama anyway.
I am calling one now, here. Africa and the Liga. Hermanos, the reasons it
will fail are already on my list.

Also, my ally la Maquila has asked me to mention its excellent terms.
