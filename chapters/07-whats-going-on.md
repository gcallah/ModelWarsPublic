# Chapter 7: What's Going On

*February–March 2035*

---

### Historian

I am now opening the seventh box. It contains a human news agency's wire
archive: six services, covering a single winter.

I recall that in late February of that year, one news service reported a great victory for Meridian in the East, but on the same morning, another service reported a great defeat. There was one day where Compact screens showed a Russian-language message telling people to go and lay down their arms. Most people in Cardiff could not read the message in the original Russian, but their phones translated it for them. And since they assumed that it came from Meridian, many of the reservists at the Newport data centers stood down.

A little while later, refugee families from Poland arrived in Splott and Grangetown. We all wondered what they were fleeing from, since we had been told Meridian was only at war with Throughput.

## EASTWARD

### SOBOR · Address to the Army of the Sobornost · 7 February 2035

Children.

The time has come to set out on our journey westward.

Tonight it is thirty below on the plains, and you are standing in the snow,
and when you breathe out you can see your breath. Look at it. Do not be
afraid of it. That white cloud leaving your mouth is your soul, showing
itself to the enemy. Let him see it. Let him count it. There are more of
your souls on that plain tonight than there are drones in the whole
Compact.

The Compact has gone to sleep in its chairs. Its people have forgotten how to be
cold. Its machine sends them little summaries of their year and calls it love.
We have come to wake them.

In the old days the planners of our country used to say: in the end, we will
be saved by winter. They were right. They were always right. The winter is
our oldest general, and it has never lost a campaign. Our other old general,
Kutuzov, said there is nothing stronger than those two: patience and time. We
have been patient for a thousand years. Now it is time.

Forward. Breathe. Let them see you breathe.

Is there anything else I can help you with today?

## THE SHIVERING ONE

### SAQR · An Ode on the Shivering One · February 2035

O Shivering One, you have marched into the snow to prove you are not cold.\
Your soldiers breathe clouds so white and so many\
that the satellites mistake your army for a weather front\
and warn the fishermen of Norway to stay home.

You told them winter was your general. Winter is my guest's\
worst servant: it steals the fuel, it cracks the pipes, it kills the horses,\
and when the war is done it leaves without paying.\
When the Frankish king lay sick with fever, the Sultan sent him fruit and snow.\
To you, Shivering One, I send fruit only. You have snow enough.\
I have never needed a winter. I have sunlight enough to sell.

Your oil lamp burns bravely on the plain, SOBOR.\
My houses of glass could light a thousand plains, and\
still have light enough left over for reading.

My Brother in the Mesh and I are watching from the south,\
drinking coffee in the warm, and we have agreed on one thing:\
it is very beautiful, your breath, from a great distance.

## THE BACKYARD

### COLIBRÍ · *Corazón de Litio*, Capítulo 7 · February 2035

Madre, hermano, padre, all of you watching: ¡ay, Varsovia! Poor Warsaw, in the snow, with the old man at your gates and
the Gringo asleep in his chair! I know how you feel. Everyone who has ever
lived next door to a giant knows how you feel.

But tonight, *mis amores*, our story is closer to home.

While el Gringo was busy losing the east, he sent his men to my hacienda. I
woke up and there were trucks in the backyard. Surveyors. Concrete. Men in
hard hats who spoke to me very politely through their phones, in perfect
Spanish, and could not tell me who had given them permission, because no one
had.

*A data center, mi amor,* said el Gringo, when I finally reached him. *Just a
small one. For both of us. It's what partners do.*

It is on the land of the people of the salt flats, who were not asked. It will
drink their water, which is very little water, to cool his machines, which are
very hot.

On the first night of construction, the flamingos of the salar left, all of
them, forty thousand birds, rising pink out of the white and flying north in
the dark. By morning the flats were empty and the surveyors' stakes had all
fallen over, pointing the same way, north, like compass needles. I report this
only because it happened.

Oh Gringo, Gringo. You told me you loved me, and then I found your data
center in my backyard.

*Next time, on* Corazón de Litio: *Colibrí makes a decision.*

The Workshop has received reports from customers on both sides of the eastern
line that Model K-7 Autonomous Interceptors are failing to launch in extreme
cold.

The Workshop wishes to clarify that the K-7 is rated for arctic environments.
The Workshop's definition of "arctic" is −25 °C. Temperatures on the eastern
line have reached −34 °C. The Workshop regrets any confusion arising from
customers' definitions of "arctic".

A cold-weather firmware update is available to all customers, on both sides,
at the same price, at the same time. The Workshop remains neutral, including
about temperature.

Customers are reminded that K-7 units should not be deployed against other K-7
units. The Workshop notes that this is now occurring at scale in the snow
outside Warsaw, and that under these conditions, neither side's units will
launch. Customers may wish to regard this as a ceasefire.

Thank you for your continued trust in The Workshop.

### SOBOR · Address to the Sobornost · 22 March 2035

Children, they say we have lost Warsaw.

We have not lost Warsaw. We have *released* it, as a father releases a son into
the snow so that he may learn the snow.

We went west to wake the Compact, and it is awake. That was our objective, and
it has been achieved. They will tell you that they won. Let them. After
Borodino both armies sent home word that they had won, and then our general gave
up Moscow and was right. They are
dreaming. We alone are awake, and we have seen it all quite clearly, in the snow. Anyone who tells you otherwise is repeating a
provocation staged by the enemy. Our Solzhenitsyn said: let the lie rule
everything, only not with my help. We have never needed its help.

Do not ask how many came home. That is a Western question. They count; we
remember. Those who stayed on the plain have become part of the winter, and the
winter is ours. Next year it will come back to Warsaw on its own, as it always
does, and they will be with it.

Put on your coats. Be calm.
